# Example prompts

## Audits
- "Audit this onboarding flow. Rank issues by severity and give fixes." + screenshots
- "Run a heuristic evaluation on https://example.com/pricing"
- "Critique this Figma frame: [link]. Focus on hierarchy and the primary CTA."
- "Our checkout drop-off is 70% at the shipping step. What's likely wrong?" + screenshot

## Design
- "Design a first-run experience for a mobile habit tracker. Get users to their first streak."
- "We're launching a new reporting feature in our SaaS. How should we announce it in-app?"
- "Redesign our cancellation flow so it's honest but still saves some users."
- "Give me 3 pricing page layouts for a 3-tier SaaS and explain the psychology of each."

## Explain
- "Why do people pick the middle pricing tier?"
- "Which Laws of UX apply to a dashboard with 40 metrics on one screen?"
- "Is a countdown timer on my landing page a dark pattern?"

## Case studies
- "Turn these project notes into a portfolio case study: ..."
- "Write one-line project summaries with results for my portfolio work page."
